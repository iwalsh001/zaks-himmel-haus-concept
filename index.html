Der Bierhaus

Dark timber, long tables, a heated patio.

Reservations are for indoors. The patio is first come, first served — and in summer the lawn and picnic tables open up.

Über Uns

A German bierhaus in the Sierra.

Himmel Haus pairs a wide range of German and Belgian biers and wines with a menu of Bavarian-inspired food — built on local dairy, organic produce, and humanely raised meat.

Our vision is to take the customary ideas and theories of German cuisine and give them a contemporary, California twist — while still putting strictly traditional recipes on the table throughout the year.
